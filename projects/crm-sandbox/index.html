HOW TO MAKE THIS A REAL CRM EXERCISE

Repeat the workflow in HubSpot

  1. Create a free account at HubSpot CRM.
  2. Add three to five fictional contacts and create deals associated with those contacts.
  3. Use stages such as New inquiry, Contacted, Qualified, Trial started, and Converted; take screenshots of the real CRM views.
  4. Create a saved welcome-email template or use the email tools available in your account. Do not assume full workflow automation is included in every plan; confirm current subscription access first.
  5. Document what you configured and which parts were unavailable in the free account. Never upload actual personal customer data to a public demo.
