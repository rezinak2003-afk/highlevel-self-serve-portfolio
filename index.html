MY APPROACH

Useful before impressive

Good support reduces uncertainty. Every project is designed around a concrete user task, clear next steps, and a way to check whether the task worked.

01. Understand

Identify the user's goal, their current step, and what is blocking them.

02. Guide

Explain the next action in plain language, with specific steps and expected results.

03. Confirm

Check whether the user reached the intended outcome and document repeatable solutions.
